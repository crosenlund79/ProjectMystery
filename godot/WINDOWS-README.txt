PROJECT MYSTERY - Windows build
================================

To play: double-click ProjectMystery.exe. Nothing else is needed: no Python, no
installer, no internet connection, no accounts or keys.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen), choose "More info" and
then "Run anyway". The game is not code-signed yet.

The game is the single file ProjectMystery.exe; you can move it anywhere. It needs a
graphics driver with OpenGL 3.3, which almost every PC from the last ten years has. If it
will not start, update the graphics driver from the PC or graphics card maker's site.

Your saved investigations and settings live in:
    %APPDATA%\ProjectMystery
Delete that folder to start completely fresh.

How to play
-----------
Choose New Case and pick a case. You arrive in the town square. Click a building on
the map to go there: talk to whoever is there, look around, read the record books kept
there, and examine the vehicles parked there. People you have met are marked on the map.

Press T (or the Tablet button) for your tablet: case notes, notebook, evidence, people,
the timeline (it flags accounts that cannot both be true), hints, and "Solve the case",
where you submit your theory and discover what really happened.

Mark what you learn Suspicious, Active or Ignore (ignored things are never deleted:
"Review ignored" brings them back). Pick "Hobby Detective" on the New Case screen to be
able to check suspicious items: you learn whether they are connected to the incident,
never who did it. Three wrong calls and the checks are gone.

Every case comes from a simulated town; nothing is scripted. Witnesses can be
mistaken, can protect people, and the person responsible may lie.
